Privacy & backup

Your food diary is stored in this browser on this device using IndexedDB. It is not uploaded to an account. Browser storage can be cleared by your browser or operating system, so export backups regularly.

Accuracy & data limitations

  • Barcode identifies a product, not the amount eaten.
  • Online product records may be missing, outdated or user-submitted.
  • Unknown nutrients are shown as “—” and are not silently counted as zero.
  • Photos are stored on your device; large images use storage space.
  • Offline logging works for foods already entered or saved. New barcode lookups need internet.
